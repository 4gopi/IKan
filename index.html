About I Kan Travel

Welcome to I Kan Travel — where every journey begins with passion

Travel with us to know your self.

Incorporated in 2024, I Kan Travel is more than a brand — it's a heartfelt vision brought to life with a deep commitment to redefining the travel experience in Sri Lanka. Founded by Iyakannu Harun, a passionate explorer whose footsteps have touched every corner of the island, the company was born from a profound love for tourism and a belief in the transformative power of travel.

Harun's journey across Sri Lanka didn't just ignite a passion — it shaped a philosophy. For him, travel is not merely movement from place to place, but a mindful experience: a way of connecting with people, culture, and nature on a deeper level. That philosophy is the heartbeat of I Kan Travel.

We go beyond sightseeing. We're here to understand the needs of travelers, not only to help them explore but to educate and inspire through authentic experiences — tourism that is inclusive, enriching, and respectful. Let us take you on a journey that doesn't just show you Sri Lanka, but lets you feel it, live it, and understand it.

Founder's note

"As a native of the beautiful Hill Country of Sri Lanka, my journey began in the structured world of banking. Over the years I served in many parts of the country, and although I always had a passion for travel, my career often left little time for it — yet I never missed a chance to explore this island whenever I could.

This island, our paradise, is truly special. Every corner tells a unique story, each place offering its own feeling, message, and soul — from misty mountains to golden beaches, ancient temples to vibrant villages. Through my travels I didn't just discover the country, I discovered myself, and found clarity about who I am and what truly matters to me.

That realization led me to a new calling: meaningful tourism. My wish is to create experiences that educate, inspire, and connect people deeply to the heart of Sri Lanka — to celebrate our land, our culture, and our identity."

Iyakannu Harun Founder, I Kan Travel
